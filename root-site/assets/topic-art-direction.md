# Signal Cartography

Financial life is a landscape of changing forces, not a wall of numbers. Each image treats one topic as a small territory: an exchange of flows, a measured horizon, a calendar of decisions, or a city whose value changes by block. The subject is suggested through its geometry before it is named.

A warm paper ground carries a disciplined system of ink, vermilion, sea-glass green, and a single topic-specific accent. Fine rules, indexed marks, and restrained chart forms recall a carefully kept field notebook. Every relationship is deliberate: a price line has a reason to bend, a calendar cell a reason to glow, a network node a reason to connect.

The images share one visual language but never one composition. Dense observations gather at one edge and resolve into open space; small labels establish scale while one strong form anchors the eye. The balance should feel calm enough for repeated reading and vivid enough to distinguish each subject at a glance.

Text remains sparse and integrated into the visual structure. Chinese titles are treated as part of the artifact, with clear hierarchy and generous breathing room. The result should feel meticulously crafted, the product of patient observation and expert refinement rather than a generic dashboard thumbnail.
